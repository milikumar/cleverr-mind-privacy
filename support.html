Support for Cleverr Mind

Need help with Cleverr Mind? Here are answers to common questions.

How do I get a perfect score?

You only need 8 out of 10 correct answers for a perfect score. Once you hit 8 correct, your timer automatically stops!

What do skips do?

You get 2 skips per quiz. Skipping reveals the correct answer without affecting your score, and even saves you 2 seconds on your timer.

Why don't my stats update when I'm offline?

When you don't have an internet connection, Cleverr Mind uses a small set of backup questions so you can keep playing. Since these questions repeat, points and stats aren't tracked for offline rounds — but your daily streak still counts.

How do I change the app's theme?

Go to Settings → App Theme to choose from 30 colors and switch between white or black text.
