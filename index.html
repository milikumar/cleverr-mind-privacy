Privacy Policy for Cleverr Mind

Last updated: August 2026

Cleverr Mind ("the app") is a trivia quiz game. This policy explains what information the app interacts with.

Information We Collect

  • Game Center: If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple provides the app with your Game Center player identifier so that scores can be submitted to leaderboards. This is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service and is subject to Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Gameplay Statistics: The app stores your quiz statistics (points, streaks, badges, category progress, and preferences) locally on your device using Apple's standard storage system. This information is not transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Trivia Questions: To provide quiz questions, the app sends requests to the Open Trivia Database (opentdb.com), a third-party trivia question service. These requests do not include any personal information beyond your device's standard network connection.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information. We do not use analytics or advertising services.

Data Storage

All of your gameplay statistics, preferences, and progress are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any server we control. Deleting the app will delete this data.

Children's Privacy

Cleverr Mind does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
